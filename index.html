FASTENER ENGINEERING

Understanding Fastener Manufacturing & Engineering

Industrial fasteners are engineered through a sequence of controlled processes designed to achieve the required dimensions, thread quality, strength, finish and reliability for their intended application.

01

Raw Material & Inspection

Fastener production begins with suitable raw material selection and inspection for quality and consistency.

02

Cold Forming / Head Forming

Metal can be shaped using forming dies to create the required fastener geometry and head profile.

03

Thread Rolling

Threads can be formed using controlled tooling to achieve the required thread profile and dimensional accuracy.

04

Heat Treatment

Depending on material and specification, heat treatment can be used to achieve required hardness, strength and toughness.

05

Finishing & Quality Testing

Finishing, dimensional checks, visual inspection and final quality verification support reliable finished fasteners.
