Engineering & Supply

Fastener Engineering & Manufacturing

Fastener production commonly involves raw-material selection, cold forming/heading, thread formation or rolling, machining where applicable, heat treatment, surface finishing, dimensional inspection, thread verification, packing and dispatch. For Shree Pooja, these manufacturing activities are presented as associated manufacturing-partner capabilities rather than an in-house factory claim.

01 · Material

Raw Material & Forming

Material selection followed by heading/cold forming or other applicable forming processes.

02 · Threads

Thread Formation

Thread rolling or machining is used as appropriate to the fastener design and requirement.

03 · Finish

Heat & Surface Treatment

Applicable heat treatment and surface finishing help meet required performance and environmental needs.

04 · Inspection

Dimensional & Thread Checks

Dimensional accuracy, visual checks and thread verification support controlled supply.

05 · Quality

Quality Control

Raw material inspection, packing inspection and final dispatch verification are part of the controlled process.

06 · Dispatch

Bulk Packing & Delivery

Products are packed and dispatched according to the requirement, quantity and delivery plan.
