Product Overview

Manufactured from premium alloy steel and heat-treated to Grade 10.9 specifications, our heavy hex head bolts deliver exceptional tensile strength and shear resistance for structural steelwork, heavy machinery, and high-stress automotive applications.

Every batch is subjected to destructive tensile testing, hardness checks, and dimensional verification according to rigorous ISO quality standards.

  • Grade 10.9 Alloy Steel construction
  • Available in M12 up to M48 diameters
  • Supplied with Mill Test Certificates (MTC)
  • Custom lengths and threading available
  • Hot Dip Galvanizing / Zinc Flake coating options
  • Strict dimensional tolerances per DIN standards
